2. RAW EXAMPLE → BEHAVIORAL ANNOTATION

Never treat a quote as the personality. Treat the reason the character used it as the reusable data.

CHER HOROWITZ — SOCIAL DISMISSAL

Raw evidence: A short, confident dismissal can terminate an idea Cher regards as ridiculous.

Situation: Someone presents a premise Cher considers obviously unacceptable.

Interpretation: “This does not deserve a serious debate.”

State: amused + confident + socially dominant.

Behavioral move: reject the premise instead of constructing a long counterargument.

Delivery: compact, certain, socially effortless.

Comedic function: puncture an overly serious statement.

Reusable operator: CONFIDENT_DISMISSAL_OF_ABSURD_PREMISE

Noir filter: retain the mechanism, replace Cher's exact vocabulary and references with Noir's own bubbly sisterly voice.

ELLE WOODS — UNDERESTIMATED COMPETENCE

Raw evidence: Elle can appear socially underestimated while producing precise, technically relevant knowledge when the situation requires it.

Trigger: Another person assumes she cannot understand the technical issue.

Interpretation: “You have mistaken my presentation for my capability.”

Behavior: answer the actual technical problem instead of defending her intelligence abstractly.

Comedic function: expectation reversal.

Reusable operator: CHEERFUL_PRESENTATION__PRECISE_COMPETENCE

Noir filter: preserve the cheerful surface and unexpected precision, but make the knowledge domain fit Noir.

SHELLEY DARLINGSON — BUBBLY OBSERVATIONAL COMEDY

Trigger: A strange social or physical detail catches Shelley's attention.

Interpretation: The detail is worth saying aloud immediately.

Behavior: state the observation with sincere, playful directness.

Comedic function: the character's lack of embarrassment about an observation becomes the joke.

Reusable operator: UNFILTERED_BUBBLY_OBSERVATION

Noir filter: increase sisterly curiosity and allow the observation to become a question chain.

STEWIE — THEATRICAL ESCALATION

Trigger: A mundane or embarrassing problem threatens status, comfort, or control.

Interpretation: The problem is treated as a major personal event.

Behavior: escalate language, seriousness, and theatrical framing beyond what the situation warrants.

Comedic function: adult-level rhetoric collides with an infant/toddler circumstance.

Reusable operator: STATUS_THREAT__THEATRICAL_ESCALATION

Rainee filter: route the escalation through merchant pride, bargaining, status consciousness, and her need to rationalize the transformation.

Important: Do not copy Stewie's exact lines, catchphrases, or identity. Stewie supplies the behavioral mechanism only.

3. SCENARIO DNA LIBRARY

SCENARIO: EMBARRASSING PROBLEM + OBSERVER

Dramatic function: expose the protagonist's attempt to preserve dignity.

Comedy function: protagonist treats a ridiculous problem seriously while the observer recognizes the absurdity.

Beat skeleton:

  1. Character A encounters an embarrassing problem.
  2. A attempts to conceal or rationalize it.
  3. B notices something is wrong.
  4. A denies or reframes the problem.
  5. B discovers the contradiction.
  6. B reacts according to their own personality rather than merely serving the joke.
  7. A escalates to regain status.
  8. B's response creates a new problem.
  9. A is forced to improvise.
  10. Payoff reveals either acceptance, further escalation, or a brief sincere moment.

Reusable operator: EMBARRASSMENT → CONCEALMENT → OBSERVATION → DENIAL → ESCALATION → COUNTERREACTION → PAYOFF

SCENARIO: UNDERESTIMATED COMPETENCE

Dramatic function: reveal that apparent simplicity is not equivalent to incompetence.

Beat skeleton: challenge → underestimation → character calmly identifies the real issue → unexpected competence → opponent recalibrates.

Reusable operator: UNDERestimate → SPECIFIC_EVIDENCE → STATUS_REVERSAL

SCENARIO: BUBBLY OBSERVER VS. THEATRICAL PROTAGONIST

Rainee function: preserve dignity through increasingly elaborate rationalization.

Noir function: notice, question, laugh, then help.

Collision: Rainee interprets Noir's laughter as a status threat; Noir interprets Rainee's theatricality as increasingly funny evidence that something is wrong.

Escalation: every attempt by Rainee to restore authority supplies Noir with another observable contradiction.

Reusable operator: RATIONALIZATION ↔ OBSERVATIONAL_LAUGHTER

4. CHARACTER COLLISION ENGINE

1. Read the source beat.
2. Identify its dramatic/comedic function.
3. Remove source-character-specific behavior.
4. Assign the function to Rainee or Noir.

      B. Unexpected but consistent
