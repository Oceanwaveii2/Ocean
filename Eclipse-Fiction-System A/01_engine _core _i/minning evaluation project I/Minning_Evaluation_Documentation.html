Mining App / Evaluation Library

Real media first. Relationships later.

This is the canonical record of the BBL/Rangiku/Rainee discussion, the supplied media, and the extraction boundary for the Eclipse Fiction System.

Current phase: determine what can be mined, preserved, indexed, and made available from real video, animation, images, captions, audio, transcripts, and visual references. The Visual Narrative Authoring App decides later how the data relates, activates, or behaves.
High-priority feature requirement: Dedicated boob and booty mining is a first-class requirement for this project. These body-region signals are central to the character designs being studied because mainstream and prestige media frequently omit, flatten, marginalize, or detach curvy body forms from personality and narrative function. The Mining App must preserve body-specific geometry, silhouette, motion, secondary motion, framing, clothing interaction, camera presentation, and comedic or dramatic use as evidence rather than treating them as optional decoration.

Four-App Boundary

The system only works if its responsibilities stay separate. Mining preserves source evidence. The Visual Editor composes it. The Fiction Engine generates new material. The Composer recomposes existing material.

MEDIA
  video, images, audio, transcripts, captions, social-post context, scripts
  ->

  repeated patterns, negative observations, provenance-aware JSON
  ->

  ->
WRITING PASS
  final prose only
The Mining App can propose observations and candidate patterns, but it must not silently turn a guess about a real person into permanent character truth. No amount of relationship logic can replace evidence that was never extracted.

Evidence Hierarchy

signal or measurement
  -> observation
  -> interpretation or inference
  -> repeated pattern
  -> author-accepted rule
  • Signal: a frame, subtitle, timecode, camera angle, caption, audio event, or posture change.
  • Observation: what the source visibly or audibly does, without assigning private motive.
  • Inference: a plausible expressive function with confidence and provenance.
  • Pattern: a recurrence across sources, not a conclusion from one moment.
  • Author-accepted rule: authored in the Visual Editor, not emitted by Mining.
Live-action social media can support a record of public-facing performance, framing, and repeated visual behavior. It cannot justify a claim that the system knows the source person's private psychology, history, or intent.

Dedicated boob, booty, and secondary-motion channels (HIGH PRIORITY)

The Mining App also has dedicated boob and booty channels. These are specialized motion and design evidence, not merely generic appearance tags. They can later connect physical form to behavior, framing, comedy, costume, or action.

  • Booty Tracking: booty/butt/peach-butt geometry, silhouette, apparent volume class, camera orientation, pelvic/lower-body displacement, sway or oscillation, turning, walking, posing, camera-facing presentation, repetition, and context.
  • Boob Tracking: chest-region geometry and orientation plus primary and secondary motion: displacement, acceleration, follow-through, phase delay, overshoot, oscillation, damping, settling, symmetry, and response to walking, running, jumping, turning, stopping, impact, or camera movement.
  • Related tracking channels: face, full body, hair, clothing, and accessories, which provide the surrounding motion chain needed to interpret a body-region event accurately.

Search and vocabulary aliases

Language is part of the retrieval problem. The interface should accept colloquial and cross-language terms as discovery aliases while normalizing results into stable schema labels. Useful booty-search terms include booty, butt, big butt, peach butt, big peach butt, BBL, Brazilian butt, bunda, and bundona. Chinese social-media searches may also use translated or platform-specific equivalents for peach-shaped or Kardashian-influenced body presentation; those terms should retain source language, platform, and translation confidence rather than being treated as universal cultural facts.

Entertainment and joke-register vocabulary

The miner must preserve the language used by entertainment itself. Body-focused characters can be built around recurring jokes, euphemisms, boasts, reactions, double meanings, absurd logistics, and self-aware commentary. English aliases may include boobs, boobies, breasts, tits, rack, pair, cans, melons, honkers, knockers, bazongas, jugs, curves, and big chest. Each term should retain register and speaker ownership.

Japanese entertainment needs its own search and translation layer: おっぱい (oppai), 胸 (mune), 巨乳 (kyonyuu), 爆乳 (bakunyuu), ボインボイン (boin-boin), and お尻 (oshiri). Preserve the Japanese spelling, romaji, translation, register, and whether the term comes from the character, another character, a narrator, caption, or fan/community label. These terms are not interchangeable.

  • Third-party remark: another character comments on the body.
  • Self-commentary: the character notices, boasts about, apologizes for, or privately acknowledges the feature.
  • Euphemistic substitution: a safer word replaces a direct body term.
  • Visual exaggeration: scale, motion, support structures, clothing failure, or impossible physics becomes the punchline.
  • Reaction beat: stare, pause, shock, envy, embarrassment, or deadpan response.
  • Recurring series gag: the feature returns as a recognizable character or world motif.

Examples such as “nice cans,” a character privately acknowledging her own figure, or an Aphrodite-style scene where attendants physically support exaggerated breasts should be mined as dialogue, self-awareness, visual exaggeration, and joke structure. The exact source, translation, speaker, and context remain attached. A joke register can influence Rainee's expressive vocabulary later, but it does not automatically define her morality, sexuality, or private psychology.

The full dedicated specification is available in Mining Video Behavior Enhancement Research. Its numeric values are schema examples unless a particular clip has been measured. Body-region events should retain source timecode, visibility or occlusion status, confidence, triggering motion, vocabulary alias, and context. The Visual Editor later decides whether a signal becomes a character trait, physical-comedy device, costume or silhouette rule, or permitted action channel.

The desired outcome is a novelized behavioral record that remains attached to the original material. An author or downstream node should always be able to return to the source file and time window.

Case Study A: Football-Practice Micro-Behavior

The football clip is a strong mining challenge because the meaningful information is small, visual, repeated, and not stated in dialogue. The system should be able to preserve a look, brief expression, posture adjustment, hand placement, hip shift, turn, deliberate walk, camera framing, and the timing between them.

Directly supportable observations

  • A football-practice setting is visible.
  • The subject is filmed in a social and camera-aware context.
  • Nearby people and the camera are part of the spatial situation.
  • The source includes a turn, posture adjustment, hip movement, and a walk toward the field.
  • Clothing, composition, and repeated framing make silhouette and public presentation relevant evidence channels.
  • A brief added, cartoon-like wobble/squeak cue is synchronized with the movement sequence. The clearest candidate moments are approximately 00:00:01.6 and 00:00:02.7; its exact sound-library source is unknown.

Audiovisual sync pass: what to look for

For each video fragment, compare visible action with the audio at the same timecode. Store whether an event is recorded scene sound, added editorial sound, music, speech, or uncertain, alongside onset, duration, repetition, confidence, and narrative function. Here the cautious label is exaggerated_comedic_wobble: a likely editorial physical-comedy accent, not a fact about private psychology.

Candidate interpretations, not private-person facts

  • Comfort with being visually observed.
  • Playful or deliberate public presentation.
  • Use of posture and movement as social communication.
  • A public visual brand expressed through recurring framing, styling, and movement.
The proposed nose-scrunch is exactly the sort of tiny event the miner should attempt to capture, but it should be stored only after a close timestamped review confirms it. The current frame pass more clearly supports the larger posture, movement, and walking sequence.

Case Study B: BBL-Dominant Rainee Dataset

Rainee is an author-created character, not an equal 50/50 blend. The BBL corpus is intentionally dominant in visible body form, silhouette, public presentation, camera-facing behavior, movement motifs, clothing emphasis, and visual branding. Rangiku Matsumoto is the anime-character substrate and Bleach-world bridge: canon relationships, narrative context, dialogue possibilities, combat setting, and existing character material.

Source-volume imbalance is not automatically a defect. Hundreds of BBL clips outweighing four to eight hours of Rangiku material is acceptable when BBL dominance is the authorial target. Mining preserves both source pools. Later authoring decides which evidence is eligible for the current context.

Case Study C: Montreal Club as Character and World

The Montreal strip-club source is special because it can live in more than one place in the authored graph without being duplicated or reduced to a single category.

  • Connected to Rainee, it can be a behavioral and transformation source.
  • Connected to a scene, it can be a World Node: location, atmosphere, collective social logic, staging, audience behavior, and comedy framing.
  • Connected to an ensemble, it can be shared scene data with performer, patron, bartender, and observer deltas.
  • Connected to a transformation, it can support the joke that Rainee becomes the club's collective or hive mind.
Montreal Club source
  -> Rainee character page: behavior and transformation reference
  -> World graph: location, social system, visual atmosphere
  -> Ensemble graph: shared scene data plus member deltas
  -> Comedy relationship: temporary hive-mind or cutaway state

This is a role-versus-identity distinction. The club is one authored source with multiple graph roles. Mining should first preserve its setup, transition, spatial layout, performers, patrons, staging, music/sound cues, money exchange, camera grammar, joke setup, joke release, and return to the surrounding narrative.

The supplied generated images are worth retaining because they reveal a present visual-model limitation: the high-level concept transfers, while identity, anatomy, exact pose, cast continuity, and multi-character spatial continuity drift. That is visual-authoring feedback. It does not replace the source video or its frames.

Visual Authoring: Recorded Here, Not Solved Here

The Visual Editor takes inspiration from Houdini, Unreal, and Cinema 4D node editing. Containers can hold nodes. Reusable groups can expose selected outputs. The same source can participate in multiple arrangements. Hierarchy provides navigation; the node graph provides composition.

A Character Node can contain a World-like source. A World Node can contain character-like agents. A relationship can expose only selected channels. The source does not need to be permanently sorted into one box before it becomes useful.

Future relationship-layer concerns

  • Context activation and scene-specific eligibility.
  • Channel-level dominance rather than global averaging.
  • Selective transfer of visual, behavioral, dialogue, or world channels.
  • Group/ensemble and hive-mind composition.
  • Nested roles and re-used sources.
  • Author overrides, ON/OFF states, and inspectable collision outputs.
  • A possible nullification function that removes source-specific identity while retaining an abstract scene structure for later reassociation.
The Bulma/Goku thought experiment belongs here, not in Mining: preserve a shonen fight's source fragments, suppress Goku-specific identity later, and make a Bulma-derived stack eligible to inhabit the abstracted action. Mining's role is to preserve the relevant fighting, dialogue, movement, setting, and female-combat evidence cleanly enough that the operation is possible.

Emergent collision is intended. Personality can emerge from graph conditions rather than a blend slider. The authoring system should expose and edit those conditions, not remove every surprise.

Mining at Collection Scale

collection
  -> source item
    -> episode, post, or file
      -> scene or post segment
        -> shot or clip window
          -> capture entry
  1. Create a manifest with source id, path, hash, duration, modality, and source class.
  2. Preserve post context separately from raw video: caption, visible post information, title, tags, series marker, and location label.
  3. Segment video, speech, and shot changes before semantic interpretation.
  4. Extract observations before inferences: subject identity, clothing, pose, movement, camera, text, dialogue, sound, and setting.
  5. Run specialized passes for behavior, dialogue/reaction pairs, visual motifs, sound, and repeated-pattern retrieval.
  6. Link recurring motifs across sources without deleting source boundaries.
  7. Record negative evidence with its search scope.
  8. Present patterns for author acceptance, revision, or rejection.

Thirty-minute processing batches are fine for throughput. They are not the identity of the evidence. Every result must return to an original source file and time window.

Data and human review

The project starts from a practical artistic problem: people have limited attention, limited source exposure, social assumptions, status filters, and emotional reactions that can make useful material invisible. A structured evidence system can force a wider search and preserve details a casual observer never consciously catalogued.

This does not make an LLM automatically objective. The operational advantage is that source selection, extraction prompts, evidence labels, confidence, provenance, transfer eligibility, and author decisions can be inspected and changed. The model expands the creator's search space; the author still decides what is meaningful.

Relevant Research and Design Implications

These sources support the technical architecture. They do not establish that a model can correctly infer a real person's private personality from media.

  • ImageBind: One Embedding Space To Bind Them All (CVPR 2023) learns a shared representation across image, text, audio, depth, thermal, and IMU data. Design implication: a future retrieval layer can find cross-modal evidence, but shared embeddings must not replace provenance or authored meaning.
  • Multimodal Transformer for Unaligned Multimodal Language Sequences (ACL 2019) addresses text, facial, and acoustic streams that do not line up perfectly. Design implication: preserve modality-specific timecodes and cross-modal links rather than forcing video, audio, captions, and transcripts into a falsely exact single clock.
  • VideoCLIP: Contrastive Pre-training for Zero-shot Video-Text Understanding (EMNLP 2021) uses temporally overlapping video/text for retrieval, action localization, and segmentation. Design implication: retrieval can locate candidate behavior, but the project still needs source fragments, confidence, and direct-versus-derived labels.
  • W3C PROV-DM: The PROV Data Model distinguishes entities, activities, agents, derivations, and collections. Design implication: source file, extraction pass, human editor, model/API, derived image, and author-approved rule can form an inspectable provenance chain instead of one opaque JSON blob.
